## Why It Matters

These updates focus on two key pillars of annotation efficiency:

1. **Longer Video Support:** Annotators can work with extended footage in a single file, removing the need to split videos before using SAM 2.
2. **Project Visibility:** Health scores, progress bars, and quick actions help teams manage projects faster and maintain data quality.

Together, these enhancements help computer vision teams label long videos with less effort and keep every project on track.

## FAQs

**Q1. How many frames can SAM 2 process in a video?**
SAM 2 can now process videos up to 5,000 frames, so you no longer need to split longer files.

**Q2. What information does the enhanced Projects workspace show?**
Each project displays a health score and a progress bar, along with quick actions for managing it.

**Q3. Can I sort and filter projects in the workspace?**
Yes, you can browse, sort, and filter projects directly in the enhanced Projects workspace.
